liteFin

Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 7, 2026

The short version: liteFin collects no personal data, sends nothing to any external server, and stores everything exclusively on your device.

What We Collect

liteFin stores the following data locally on your Apple TV only:

  • Your Jellyfin server URL and access token, used to authenticate with your server
  • App preferences (streaming quality, autoplay, skip intro toggle)
  • Your local watchlist

What We Don't Collect

We do not collect, transmit, or store any of the following:

  • Personal information of any kind
  • Viewing history or activity
  • Jellyfin account information beyond what is needed to connect
  • Analytics or usage data
  • Crash reports or diagnostics

Your Jellyfin Server

liteFin connects exclusively to the Jellyfin server you provide. All media, metadata, and playback activity is handled directly between your Apple TV and your server. We have no access to your server or its data.

Third-Party Services

liteFin contains no analytics, advertising networks, or tracking services of any kind.

Children's Privacy

liteFin does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

Changes to This Policy

If we make changes to this privacy policy, we will update the date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after any changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
